Activity 1 – Choose the correct option

Choose the expression that best completes each sentence.

1. The new sports centre will cost __________ €8 million to build.

3. The journey should take three hours, __________.
4. The company employs __________ 1,500 people across its international offices.
5. The repairs will cost __________ €2,000, so we need to make sure we have enough money.
6. The meeting lasted an hour __________ before everyone finally reached an agreement.
7. The apartment can accommodate __________ six people.
8. The university has received __________ 10,000 applications this year.
9. The figures are __________ the same as those recorded last year.
10. The temperature should remain at around 20°C, __________.
